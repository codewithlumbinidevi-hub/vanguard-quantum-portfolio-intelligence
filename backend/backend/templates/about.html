About the Platform

This frontend is designed for a production-grade quantum-enhanced portfolio optimization workflow that can sit on top of a Flask backend. It presents institutional-grade views for portfolio construction, optimization execution, results analysis, and risk interpretation without implementing the numerical optimization itself.

The experience is tailored for allocators and research teams evaluating multi-asset strategies, realistic constraints, and advanced scenario analysis.

Platform Capabilities
  • Flask-compatible route structure
  • Bootstrap-based responsive UI
  • Chart.js visualizations
  • Fetch-based API integration scaffolding
